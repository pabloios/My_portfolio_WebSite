# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, security team leads, and technical founders evaluating Pablo Jose Sarmiento Moreno for junior cybersecurity, SOC analyst, automation, agentic engineering, or software roles. They need a quick, credible picture of his technical direction, work history, certifications, and contact paths.

## Product Purpose
Present Pablo as a serious, business-ready technology professional with a focused path into cybersecurity, security operations, automation, and AI-assisted engineering. Success means a visitor can understand his profile in under one minute, inspect his trajectory, and move directly to GitHub, LinkedIn, email, or the CV.

## Brand Personality
Precise, resilient, operational. The tone should feel like a security operations room translated into an executive portfolio: calm under pressure, technical, accountable, and forward-looking.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic developer portfolio tropes, playful hacker aesthetics, neon-on-black cyber cliches, resume scans pasted into a webpage, beige template minimalism, and empty marketing claims without evidence from the CV.

## Design Principles
- Lead with trust signals: name, role, location, GitHub, LinkedIn, CV, and certifications should be immediately visible.
- Make the career transition legible: logistics, operations, QA, automation, and cybersecurity should read as a coherent operating background.
- Show capability through concrete systems: SIEM, logs, incident response, scripting, QA, CRM automation, and process optimization.
- Keep the interface executive: dense enough for evaluation, polished enough for first impression, readable on mobile.
- Use motion and color as signal, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible links, reduced-motion fallbacks, semantic sections, clear Spanish copy, and responsive layout for mobile recruiters.
